cookie

Younger than you think
First recorded
1703

≈ 323 years old

As first written

“a small flat cake — Dutch koekje, “little cake””

How it drifted

Dutch settlers’ koekje Americanized to cookie. Britain kept “biscuit” (twice-cooked); the browser kind (1994) is a “magic cookie” from programmer slang.

For perspective

That makes cookie younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 139 years.
