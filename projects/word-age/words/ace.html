ace

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1300

≈ 726 years old

As first written

“the one at dice — the worst possible roll”

How it drifted

From Latin as, the smallest coin. The lowest throw at dice became, in card games where one is high, the best card — and by WWI, the best pilot.

For perspective

That makes ace older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 192 years.

Marginalia

Ace flipped from “worthless” to “excellent” because card players decided one beats everything.
