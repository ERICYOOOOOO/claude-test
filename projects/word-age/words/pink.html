pink

Younger than you think
First recorded
1733

≈ 293 years old

As first written

“the color of the pink — a garden flower (Dianthus)”

How it drifted

The flower was called a pink from the 1570s; only in the 1730s did its blush become a color word. Before that, English just said “pale red.”

For perspective

That makes pink younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 169 years.

Marginalia

English had no word for pink until the 18th century.
