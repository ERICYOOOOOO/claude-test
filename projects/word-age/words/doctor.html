doctor

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1300

≈ 726 years old

As first written

“a church father; a teacher”

How it drifted

From Latin docēre “to teach.” Doctors of the Church came first; medicine borrowed the learned title in the later Middle Ages.

For perspective

That makes doctor older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 192 years.
