noon

Older than you think
First recorded
Old English

≈ 1126 years old

As first written

“the ninth hour — about 3 p.m.”

How it drifted

Old English nōn, from Latin nona hora, the ninth hour of daylight. Monastic prayers (and lunches) crept earlier until noon settled at twelve.

For perspective

That makes noon older than the printing press (1440) by 540 years.

Marginalia

Noon used to be mid-afternoon. The meal moved, and the word moved with it.
