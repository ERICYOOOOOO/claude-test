jeopardy

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1300

≈ 726 years old

As first written

“an evenly divided game — jeu parti”

How it drifted

Old French jeu parti, a “divided game” with even chances, was a chess term. Even odds shaded into peril: when the game could go either way, so could you.

For perspective

That makes jeopardy older than the printing press (1440) by 140 years.
