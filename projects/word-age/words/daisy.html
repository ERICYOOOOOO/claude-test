daisy

Older than you think
First recorded
Old English

≈ 1126 years old

As first written

“day’s eye — the flower that opens at dawn”

How it drifted

Old English dægesēage, “day’s eye”: it opens with the sun and closes at dusk. The poetry fossilized into a name.

For perspective

That makes daisy older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 592 years.
