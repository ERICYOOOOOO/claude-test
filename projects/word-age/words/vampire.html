vampire

Younger than you think
First recorded
1732

≈ 294 years old

As first written

“a blood-drinking revenant of Slavic folklore”

How it drifted

English learned the word during the 1732 “vampire panic,” when Serbian exhumations made European newspapers. Dracula (1897) merely gave it a wardrobe.

For perspective

That makes vampire younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 168 years.

Marginalia

“Vampire” entered English via 1730s tabloid coverage of actual exhumations.
