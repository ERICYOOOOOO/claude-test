vaccine

Younger than you think
First recorded
1799

≈ 227 years old

As first written

“of cows — Latin vacca; matter from cowpox”

How it drifted

Jenner used cowpox (variolae vaccinae, “smallpox of the cow”) to protect against smallpox. Every vaccine since is named after a cow.

For perspective

That makes vaccine younger than US independence (1776) by 23 years.

Marginalia

Vaccine literally means “from a cow” — in honor of the original cowpox inoculation.
