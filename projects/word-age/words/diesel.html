diesel

Younger than you think
First recorded
1894

≈ 132 years old

As first written

“Rudolf Diesel’s engine”

How it drifted

Diesel patented his compression-ignition engine in the 1890s, then vanished from a Channel steamer in 1913. The engine, and the name, kept running.

For perspective

That makes diesel younger than US independence (1776) by 118 years.
