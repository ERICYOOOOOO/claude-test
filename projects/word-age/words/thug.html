thug

Younger than you think
First recorded
1810

≈ 216 years old

As first written

“a member of the Thuggee — robbers who strangled travelers”

How it drifted

Hindi ṭhag, “swindler, thief.” British accounts of the (partly mythologized) Thuggee cult fixed the word; it generalized to any brute within decades.

For perspective

That makes thug younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 246 years.
