punk

Older than you think
First recorded
1590s

≈ 431 years old

As first written

“a prostitute”

How it drifted

Elizabethan slang, origin unknown — Shakespeare used it. It cycled through “rotten wood,” “worthless person,” “petty criminal,” then 1970s music made decay a badge.

For perspective

That makes punk younger than the printing press (1440) by 155 years.

Marginalia

Punk is in Measure for Measure. Shakespeare got there nearly four centuries before the Sex Pistols.
