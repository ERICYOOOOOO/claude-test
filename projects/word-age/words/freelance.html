freelance

Younger than you think
First recorded
1820

≈ 206 years old

As first written

“a medieval mercenary — a “free lance” for hire”

How it drifted

Coined by Walter Scott in Ivanhoe for soldiers whose lances served any paymaster. The gig economy is a knight errant with worse armor.

For perspective

That makes freelance younger than US independence (1776) by 44 years.

Marginalia

“Freelancer” was invented in a novel, describing mercenaries.
