dinner

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1300

≈ 726 years old

As first written

“the first big meal of the day — etymologically “breakfast””

How it drifted

From Old French disner, ultimately “to break the fast.” Dinner was late morning; over centuries the main meal — and its name — marched toward evening.

For perspective

That makes dinner older than the printing press (1440) by 140 years.

Marginalia

Dinner literally means breakfast. The meal just kept getting later.
