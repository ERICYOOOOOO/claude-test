sabotage

Younger than you think
First recorded
1907

≈ 119 years old

As first written

“deliberate bungling by workers — from sabot, “wooden shoe””

How it drifted

French sabot “clog” → saboter, “to work clumsily” (as clog-wearing rustics were said to). The tale of shoes thrown into machinery is folklore; the clumsy-work metaphor is real.

For perspective

That makes sabotage younger than the light bulb (1879) by 28 years.
