jealous

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1200

≈ 826 years old

As first written

“zealous, ardent, devoted”

How it drifted

Jealous and zealous are the same Greek zēlos split in two. Devotion that guards too hard became the green-eyed sense.

For perspective

That makes jealous older than the printing press (1440) by 240 years.
