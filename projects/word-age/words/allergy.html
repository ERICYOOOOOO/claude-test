allergy

Younger than you think
First recorded
1911

≈ 115 years old

As first written

“altered reactivity — Greek allos + ergon, “other working””

How it drifted

Coined in German (1906) by pediatrician Clemens von Pirquet for changed immune response. Now covers everything from pollen to your least favorite chore.

For perspective

That makes allergy younger than the light bulb (1879) by 32 years.
