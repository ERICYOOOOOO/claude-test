veranda

Younger than you think
First recorded
1711

≈ 315 years old

As first written

“an open pillared gallery — Hindi varandā, via Portuguese”

How it drifted

A word the colonizers and the colonized traded back and forth (Portuguese varanda, Hindi barāmdā). The architecture and the etymology are both hybrids.

For perspective

That makes veranda younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 219 years.
