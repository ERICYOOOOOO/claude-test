potato

Older than you think
First recorded
1560s

≈ 461 years old

As first written

“the sweet potato”

How it drifted

From Taíno batata via Spanish. For decades “potato” meant the sweet kind; the white Andean tuber arrived later and quietly took over the name.

For perspective

That makes potato younger than the printing press (1440) by 125 years.

Marginalia

The original potato was the sweet potato. The ordinary spud is the impostor.
