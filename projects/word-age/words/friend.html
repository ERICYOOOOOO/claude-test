friend

Older than you think
First recorded
Old English

≈ 1126 years old

As first written

“one who loves — literally “loving one””

How it drifted

Old English frēond is the present participle of frēogan “to love”: a friend is, grammatically, someone busy loving you.

For perspective

That makes friend older than Oxford University (c. 1096) by 196 years.

Marginalia

“Unfriend” is not a Facebook invention — as a noun meaning “enemy,” it appears in Middle English centuries earlier.
