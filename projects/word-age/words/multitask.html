multitask

Younger than you think
First recorded
1966

≈ 60 years old

As first written

“a computer running several tasks at once”

How it drifted

Born as computing jargon for processors; applied to humans by the 1990s. The chips got better at it; the research says we didn’t.

For perspective

That makes multitask younger than sliced bread (1928) by 38 years.
