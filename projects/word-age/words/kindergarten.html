kindergarten

Younger than you think
First recorded
1852

≈ 174 years old

As first written

“a children’s garden — Friedrich Fröbel’s coinage”

How it drifted

German Kindergarten: children were to grow like plants, tended not drilled. English took the word whole, then paved the garden with phonics.

For perspective

That makes kindergarten younger than US independence (1776) by 76 years.
