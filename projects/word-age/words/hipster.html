hipster

Younger than you think
First recorded
1941

≈ 85 years old

As first written

“one who is hip — a jazz aficionado”

How it drifted

From hip/hep, jazz slang of obscure origin. The 1940s hipster followed bebop; the 2000s revival followed vinyl and beards. Both insisted they weren’t one.

For perspective

That makes hipster younger than the sinking of the Titanic (1912) by 29 years.
