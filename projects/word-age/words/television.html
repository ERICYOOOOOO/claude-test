television

Younger than you think
First recorded
1907

≈ 119 years old

As first written

“far-seeing — a proposed technology, not yet real”

How it drifted

Greek tēle “far” + Latin visio “seeing”: named in scientific speculation about 20 years before working demonstrations. C.P. Scott sniffed: “no good will come of a word half Greek, half Latin.”

For perspective

That makes television younger than the light bulb (1879) by 28 years.
