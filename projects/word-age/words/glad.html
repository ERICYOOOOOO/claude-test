Old English

≈ 1126 years old

As first written

“bright, shining”

How it drifted

Old English glæd meant radiant — kin to Latin glaber “smooth.” Shining faces became happy ones.

For perspective

That makes glad older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 592 years.
