As first written

How it drifted

Ward Cunningham named his WikiWikiWeb after Honolulu airport’s Wiki Wiki shuttle bus. Wikipedia made the Hawaiian adverb a global noun.

For perspective

That makes wiki younger than the sinking of the Titanic (1912) by 83 years.
