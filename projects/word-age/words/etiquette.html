etiquette

Younger than you think
First recorded
1750

≈ 276 years old

As first written

“a ticket or label — the little card of court rules”

How it drifted

French étiquette, “label, ticket”: by tradition, the cards of ceremony rules at Versailles. Manners are named after the paperwork.

For perspective

That makes etiquette younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 186 years.
