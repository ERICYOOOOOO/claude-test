boycott

Younger than you think
First recorded
1880

≈ 146 years old

As first written

“to ostracize as Irish tenants did Captain Boycott”

How it drifted

Land agent Charles Boycott evicted tenants in 1880; the community froze him out entirely. Within months his surname was a verb in newspapers worldwide.

For perspective

That makes boycott younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 388 years.

Marginalia

Captain Boycott was boycotted so effectively his name became the word — in under a year.
