fairy

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1300

≈ 726 years old

As first written

“enchantment; the land of the fays”

How it drifted

Old French faerie meant the magic itself, or the realm where it operated. The country shrank into its inhabitants — one tiny winged person at a time.

For perspective

That makes fairy older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 192 years.
