basketball

Younger than you think
First recorded
1892

≈ 134 years old

As first written

“Naismith’s game — a ball and two peach baskets”

How it drifted

James Naismith nailed peach baskets to a gym balcony in December 1891 and wrote the rules; the name appeared with them. The baskets kept their bottoms for years.

For perspective

That makes basketball younger than the telephone patent (1876) by 16 years.
