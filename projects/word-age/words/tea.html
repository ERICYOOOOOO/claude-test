tea

Older than you think
First recorded
1650s

≈ 371 years old

As first written

“the leaf and drink — Min Chinese te”

How it drifted

Coastal Min traders said “te,” so Western Europe drinks tea; overland routes heard Mandarin “cha,” so Russia and India drink chai. The word maps the trade route.

For perspective

That makes tea younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 91 years.

Marginalia

Whether a country says “tea” or “chai” tells you if the leaves arrived by sea or by land.
