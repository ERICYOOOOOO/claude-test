bully

Older than you think
First recorded
1530s

≈ 491 years old

As first written

“sweetheart, darling — a term of endearment”

How it drifted

Probably from Dutch boel, “lover.” From darling to fine fellow to swaggering ruffian in about a century — the fastest heel turn in English.

For perspective

That makes bully younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 43 years.

Marginalia

“Bully for you!” preserves the old good bully — as does Roosevelt’s “bully pulpit.”
