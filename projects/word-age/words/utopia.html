utopia

Older than you think
First recorded
1516

≈ 510 years old

As first written

““no-place” — Thomas More’s invented island”

How it drifted

More coined it from Greek ou “not” + topos “place”: the perfect society that, by name, cannot exist. The pun is usually forgotten by the people planning one.

For perspective

That makes utopia younger than the Black Death reaching England (1348) by 168 years.

Marginalia

Utopia literally means “nowhere.” The joke was built in from day one.
