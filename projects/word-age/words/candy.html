candy

Older than you think
First recorded
late 13c.

≈ 746 years old

As first written

“crystallized sugar — sugre-candy”

How it drifted

Via French and Arabic qandī from a Persian word for cane sugar. It entered English glued to “sugar” and later broke free.

For perspective

That makes candy older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 212 years.
