serendipity

Younger than you think
First recorded
1754

≈ 272 years old

As first written

“the faculty of making happy accidental discoveries”

How it drifted

Coined in a letter by Horace Walpole, after the fairy tale “The Three Princes of Serendip” (Serendip = Sri Lanka), whose heroes kept stumbling on lucky finds.

For perspective

That makes serendipity younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 190 years.

Marginalia

One man invented “serendipity” in a private letter. It stayed rare until the 20th century.
