boy

Older than you think
First recorded
mid-13c.

≈ 776 years old

As first written

“a servant; a commoner, a knave”

How it drifted

First a word for male servants and rascals, of much-debated origin. Only in later Middle English did it settle on male children.

For perspective

That makes boy older than the printing press (1440) by 190 years.
