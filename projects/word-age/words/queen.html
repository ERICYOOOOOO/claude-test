queen

Older than you think
First recorded
Old English

≈ 1126 years old

As first written

“a woman; a wife”

How it drifted

Old English cwēn was woman or wife — cousin to Greek gynē “woman.” English promoted its everyday word for woman to the throne.

For perspective

That makes queen older than Oxford University (c. 1096) by 196 years.
