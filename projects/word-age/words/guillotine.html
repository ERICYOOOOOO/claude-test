guillotine

Younger than you think
First recorded
1793

≈ 233 years old

As first written

“the beheading machine urged by Dr. Guillotin”

How it drifted

Dr. Joseph-Ignace Guillotin merely advocated a painless, equal method of execution. He didn’t invent it and his family petitioned to rename it. They failed.

For perspective

That makes guillotine younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 229 years.
