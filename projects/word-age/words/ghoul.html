ghoul

Younger than you think
First recorded
1786

≈ 240 years old

As first written

“a corpse-eating desert demon — Arabic ghūl”

How it drifted

From Arabic ghūl, the grave-haunting demon of the Thousand and One Nights; Beckford’s gothic novel Vathek carried it into English.

For perspective

That makes ghoul younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 294 years.
