meander

Older than you think
First recorded
1570s

≈ 451 years old

As first written

“the Maeander — a winding river in Asia Minor”

How it drifted

The river Maiandros curved so extravagantly that Greek made it a common noun. One river’s indecision became every wanderer’s verb.

For perspective

That makes meander younger than the printing press (1440) by 135 years.
