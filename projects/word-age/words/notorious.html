notorious

Older than you think
First recorded
1540s

≈ 478 years old

As first written

“well-known, a matter of public knowledge — good or bad”

How it drifted

From Latin notus, “known.” It was neutral fame; constant pairing with criminals (“notorious thief”) stained it permanently.

For perspective

That makes notorious younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 56 years.
