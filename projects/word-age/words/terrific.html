terrific

Older than you think
First recorded
1660s

≈ 361 years old

As first written

“causing terror, frightening”

How it drifted

Latin terrificus, “dread-making.” Like awful before it, hyperbole bleached the horror: by the 1880s terrific meant excellent. Terrifying kept the old job.

For perspective

That makes terrific younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 101 years.
