ambulance

Younger than you think
First recorded
1809

≈ 217 years old

As first written

“a mobile field hospital — French hôpital ambulant”

How it drifted

“Walking hospital”: Napoleonic flying field units that followed the armies. The hospital shrank to a single vehicle and kept the adjective.

For perspective

That makes ambulance younger than US independence (1776) by 33 years.
