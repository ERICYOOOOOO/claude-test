salad

Older than you think
First recorded
late 14c.

≈ 646 years old

As first written

“salted vegetables — herba salata”

How it drifted

Vulgar Latin herba salata, “salted greens” — Romans brined their raw vegetables. Salt hides inside salad, sauce, sausage, and salsa alike.

For perspective

That makes salad older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 112 years.
