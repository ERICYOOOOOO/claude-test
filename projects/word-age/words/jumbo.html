jumbo

Younger than you think
First recorded
1882

≈ 144 years old

As first written

“the name of P.T. Barnum’s celebrity elephant”

How it drifted

Jumbo, London Zoo’s African elephant (possibly from Swahili jumbe, “chief”), was sold to Barnum in 1882 amid national outcry. His name became a size.

For perspective

That makes jumbo younger than US independence (1776) by 106 years.

Marginalia

Jumbo jets are named after one specific Victorian elephant.
