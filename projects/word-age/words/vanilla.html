vanilla

Older than you think
First recorded
1660s

≈ 361 years old

As first written

“the little pod — Spanish vainilla”

How it drifted

Spanish vainilla, “small sheath” (Latin vagina, “sheath”). The orchid pod’s name; “plain vanilla” as “boring default” is a 20th-century judgment the Aztecs would dispute.

For perspective

That makes vanilla younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 101 years.
