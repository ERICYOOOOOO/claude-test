scientist

Younger than you think
First recorded
1834

≈ 192 years old

As first written

“a cultivator of science — coined by William Whewell”

How it drifted

Coined (half-jokingly, on the model of “artist”) because “natural philosopher” was too long. Newton died a century before anyone could call him a scientist.

For perspective

That makes scientist younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 342 years.

Marginalia

Darwin sailed on the Beagle before the word “scientist” existed.
