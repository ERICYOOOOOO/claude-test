banter

Younger than you think
First recorded
1670s

≈ 351 years old

As first written

“to ridicule, to make a victim of”

How it drifted

London street slang of unknown origin; Swift condemned it in print. It softened from targeted mockery to friendly volleys.

For perspective

That makes banter younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 111 years.
