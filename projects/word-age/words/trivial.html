First recorded
1580s

≈ 441 years old

As first written

“commonplace — found at the crossroads”

How it drifted

Latin trivium, “where three roads meet”: the public square. Crossroads talk was ordinary talk. The lower university curriculum (the trivium) reinforced the slight.

For perspective

That makes trivial younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 93 years.
