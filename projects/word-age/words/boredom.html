boredom

Younger than you think
First recorded
1852

≈ 174 years old

As first written

“the state of being bored”

How it drifted

The verb bore appears only in the 1760s (origin unknown), and boredom debuts in Dickens’s Bleak House. What did people feel before? They lacked the word, at least.

For perspective

That makes boredom younger than US independence (1776) by 76 years.

Marginalia

“Boredom” is younger than the bicycle design it predates by little — Jane Austen never had the word.
