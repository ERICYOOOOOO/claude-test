machine

Older than you think
First recorded
1540s

≈ 481 years old

As first written

“a structure, a contrivance of any kind”

How it drifted

Greek mēkhanē, “device, means.” Stage machinery (deus ex machina) and siege works came first; the engine sense assembled itself in the 1600s.

For perspective

That makes machine younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 53 years.
