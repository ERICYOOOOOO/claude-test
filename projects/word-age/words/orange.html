orange

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1300

≈ 726 years old

As first written

“the fruit — the color had no name yet”

How it drifted

Via Old French and Arabic nāranj from Persian. The color sense appears only in the 1540s: before that, English called the hue “yellow-red” (ġeolurēad).

For perspective

That makes orange older than the printing press (1440) by 140 years.

Marginalia

The fruit named the color, not the other way around.
