alcohol

Older than you think
First recorded
1540s

≈ 481 years old

As first written

“a fine powder — especially kohl eyeliner”

How it drifted

Arabic al-kuḥl: powdered antimony for darkening eyelids. Alchemists extended it to any purified essence — “alcohol of wine” — and the drink took the word.

For perspective

That makes alcohol younger than the printing press (1440) by 105 years.

Marginalia

Alcohol originally meant eye makeup.
