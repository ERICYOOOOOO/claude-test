journal

Older than you think
First recorded
mid-14c.

≈ 671 years old

As first written

“a service book of daytime prayers”

How it drifted

From Latin diurnalis “daily.” A book of daily offices, then a daily record, then a newspaper, then your diary — always the same word for “day.”

For perspective

That makes journal older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 137 years.
