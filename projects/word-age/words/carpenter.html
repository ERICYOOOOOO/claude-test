carpenter

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1300

≈ 726 years old

As first written

“a wagon-maker — Latin carpentum, “two-wheeled carriage””

How it drifted

Late Latin carpentarius made carriages. The trade widened from wagons to all woodwork; the carriage still sits in the name.

For perspective

That makes carpenter older than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 264 years.
