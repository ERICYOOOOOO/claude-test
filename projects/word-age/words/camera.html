camera

Younger than you think
First recorded
1708

≈ 318 years old

As first written

“a vaulted room — the camera obscura, “dark chamber””

How it drifted

Latin camera, “vaulted chamber.” The camera obscura was a darkened room that projected the outside world; the room shrank into a box, then a phone.

For perspective

That makes camera younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 216 years.

Marginalia

A camera is, literally, a room. Early photographers stood inside theirs.
