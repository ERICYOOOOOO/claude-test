gorilla

Younger than you think
First recorded
1847

≈ 179 years old

As first written

“a “wild hairy person” from an ancient Greek travel account”

How it drifted

Named by naturalist Thomas Savage from Gorillai, “hairy women” in the Greek record of Hanno’s West African voyage (5th century BC). A 2,300-year-old rumor, reused.

For perspective

That makes gorilla younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 283 years.
