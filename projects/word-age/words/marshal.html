marshal

Older than you think
First recorded
mid-13c.

≈ 776 years old

As first written

“a horse-servant; a groom”

How it drifted

From Frankish for “horse-servant.” Like constable, a stable job that galloped up the ranks to field marshal.

For perspective

That makes marshal older than the printing press (1440) by 190 years.
