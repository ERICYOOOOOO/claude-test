As first written

“burnsides — the whiskers of General Burnside”

How it drifted

Civil War general Ambrose Burnside wore spectacular cheek whiskers; “burnsides” flipped, syllable by syllable, into sideburns.

For perspective

That makes sideburns younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 323 years.
