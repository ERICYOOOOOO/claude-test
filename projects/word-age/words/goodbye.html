goodbye

Older than you think
First recorded
1570s

≈ 451 years old

As first written

“a contraction of “God be with ye””

How it drifted

Godbwye and similar squeezes appear in the 1570s; “good” crept in by analogy with good day and good night. Every farewell is a worn-down blessing.

For perspective

That makes goodbye younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 83 years.
