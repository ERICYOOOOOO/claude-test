marathon

Younger than you think
First recorded
1896

≈ 130 years old

As first written

“the long race honoring the run from Marathon”

How it drifted

Invented for the first modern Olympics (1896) after the legend of Pheidippides’s run from Marathon to Athens — a story stitched together centuries after the battle.

For perspective

That makes marathon younger than the light bulb (1879) by 17 years.
