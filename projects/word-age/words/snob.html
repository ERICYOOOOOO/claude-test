snob

Younger than you think
First recorded
1781

≈ 245 years old

As first written

“a shoemaker; a cobbler’s apprentice”

How it drifted

Cambridge slang used it for townsmen (non-students), then for social climbers who aped their betters; Thackeray’s Book of Snobs (1848) flipped it to those looking down.

For perspective

That makes snob younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 217 years.

Marginalia

A snob was originally at the bottom of the ladder, not sneering from the top.
