dord

Younger than you think
First recorded
1934

≈ 92 years old

As first written

“a ghost word — a dictionary error meaning “density””

How it drifted

Webster’s staff misread a slip reading “D or d” (abbreviations for density) as one word: Dord. It sat in the dictionary for five years before anyone noticed.

For perspective

That makes dord younger than the Wright brothers’ first flight (1903) by 31 years.

Marginalia

Dord never existed. It’s the most famous accidental word in lexicography — printed in Webster’s from 1934 to 1939.
