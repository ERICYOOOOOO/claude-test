As first written

“a ball of thread (a spelling of “clew”)”

How it drifted

Theseus escaped the Labyrinth by unwinding a clew of thread. Following the thread out of a maze made the ball of yarn a metaphor for every solved mystery.

For perspective

That makes clue younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 31 years.

Marginalia
