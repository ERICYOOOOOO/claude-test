knave

Older than you think
First recorded
Old English

≈ 1126 years old

As first written

“a boy; a male servant”

How it drifted

Old English cnafa was just “boy.” Where knight rose, knave sank — by the Middle Ages it meant a rogue, and it dealt itself into the card deck.

For perspective

That makes knave older than Oxford University (c. 1096) by 196 years.
