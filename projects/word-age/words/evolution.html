evolution

Older than you think
First recorded
1620s

≈ 401 years old

As first written

“an unrolling — of a scroll, or troops in formation”

How it drifted

Latin evolutio, the unrolling of a book-scroll. Biology adopted the unrolling for species; Darwin himself barely used the word in early editions.

For perspective

That makes evolution younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 133 years.
