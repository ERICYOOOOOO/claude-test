charisma

Younger than you think
First recorded
1875

≈ 151 years old

As first written

“a divine gift of grace — Greek kharisma”

How it drifted

A theological term for gifts of the Spirit (healing, prophecy). Sociologist Max Weber secularized it for magnetic authority; politics has been chasing it since.

For perspective

That makes charisma younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 383 years.
