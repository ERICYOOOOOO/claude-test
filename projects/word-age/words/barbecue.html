barbecue

Older than you think
First recorded
1650s

≈ 371 years old

As first written

“a raised wooden frame for smoking or drying meat”

How it drifted

Taíno barbacoa: the rack itself. The furniture became the cooking method, then the party, then a verb, then a sauce aisle.

For perspective

That makes barbecue younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 91 years.
