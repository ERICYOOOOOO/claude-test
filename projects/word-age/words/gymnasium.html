gymnasium

Older than you think
First recorded
1590s

≈ 431 years old

As first written

“a place to exercise naked — Greek gymnos, “naked””

How it drifted

Greek athletes trained nude; the building was named for the dress code. German schools took the academic sense, English gyms the sweat.

For perspective

That makes gymnasium younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 31 years.
