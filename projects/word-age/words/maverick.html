maverick

Younger than you think
First recorded
1867

≈ 159 years old

As first written

“an unbranded calf — after rancher Samuel Maverick”

How it drifted

Texan Samuel Maverick declined to brand his cattle, so strays were called mavericks. The unbranded calf became the unbranded politician.

For perspective

That makes maverick younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 303 years.
