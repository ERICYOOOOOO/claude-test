suede

Younger than you think
First recorded
1859

≈ 167 years old

As first written

“Swedish leather — gants de Suède, “gloves of Sweden””

How it drifted

French for “Sweden”: the soft napped gloves were a Swedish specialty. The country name rubbed off onto the finish itself.

For perspective

That makes suede exactly as old as Darwin’s On the Origin of Species (1859).
