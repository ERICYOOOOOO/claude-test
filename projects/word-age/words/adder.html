adder

Older than you think
First recorded
Old English

≈ 1126 years old

As first written

“a snake — originally “a nædre””

How it drifted

Old English nædre meant snake. In Middle English “a nadder” was mis-split as “an adder,” and the n fell off — the snake shed its first letter.

For perspective

That makes adder older than Oxford University (c. 1096) by 196 years.
