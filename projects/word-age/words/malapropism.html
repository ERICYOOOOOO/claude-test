malapropism

Younger than you think
First recorded
1826

≈ 200 years old

As first written

“a word misused à la Mrs. Malaprop”

How it drifted

From Sheridan’s 1775 comedy The Rivals, whose Mrs. Malaprop (French mal à propos) says “allegory” for “alligator.” The character became the category.

For perspective

That makes malapropism younger than US independence (1776) by 50 years.
