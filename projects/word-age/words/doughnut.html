doughnut

Younger than you think
First recorded
1809

≈ 217 years old

As first written

“a fried dough ball — literally a “nut” of dough”

How it drifted

Washington Irving described “balls of sweetened dough, fried in hog’s fat, and called dough-nuts.” The hole arrived mid-century; “donut” is 1920s marketing.

For perspective

That makes doughnut younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 245 years.
