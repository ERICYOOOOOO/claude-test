As first written

“a little mouse — Latin musculus”

How it drifted

Romans thought a flexing biceps looked like a mouse running under the skin, so they called it musculus, “little mouse.” Anatomy agreed.

For perspective

That makes muscle older than the printing press (1440) by 60 years.
