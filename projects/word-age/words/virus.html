virus

Older than you think
First recorded
late 14c.

≈ 646 years old

As first written

“poison, venom; a corrosive fluid”

How it drifted

Latin virus, “poison, slime.” It named venoms for five centuries, was adopted for infectious agents in the 1890s, and jumped to computers in the 20th century.

For perspective

That makes virus older than the printing press (1440) by 60 years.
