tornado

Older than you think
First recorded
1550s

≈ 471 years old

As first written

“a violent tropical thunderstorm”

How it drifted

Sailors’ mangling of Spanish tronada, “thunderstorm” (tronar, to thunder), later reshaped by tornar, “to turn.” The spin was added by folk etymology before meteorology confirmed it.

For perspective

That makes tornado younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 63 years.
