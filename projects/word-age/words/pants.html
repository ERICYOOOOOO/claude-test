pants

Younger than you think
First recorded
1840

≈ 186 years old

As first written

“pantaloons, clipped — from a Venetian clown”

How it drifted

Pantaloons trace to Pantalone, the commedia dell’arte’s skinny-legged old fool. Trimmed to pants in America; Britain reassigned the word to underwear.

For perspective

That makes pants younger than US independence (1776) by 64 years.

Marginalia

Pants descend from the name of an Italian comedy character.
