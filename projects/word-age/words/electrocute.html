electrocute

Younger than you think
First recorded
1889

≈ 137 years old

As first written

“to execute by electricity”

How it drifted

Electro- + execute, coined for the new electric chair (first used 1890). The accidental sense came later; the word began as a death sentence.

For perspective

That makes electrocute exactly as old as the Eiffel Tower (1889).
