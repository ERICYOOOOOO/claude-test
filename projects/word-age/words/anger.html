anger

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1200

≈ 826 years old

As first written

“distress, affliction, grief”

How it drifted

From Old Norse angr “grief, sorrow” — kin to “anguish” and “anxious.” The suffering turned outward and became rage.

For perspective

That makes anger older than the printing press (1440) by 240 years.
