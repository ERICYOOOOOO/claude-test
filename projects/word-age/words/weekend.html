weekend

Older than you think
First recorded
1630s

≈ 388 years old

As first written

“the end of the week”

How it drifted

Attested from the 1630s, but rare until northern English factory culture (and later the two-day rest) made it an institution in the 1800s.

For perspective

That makes weekend younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 74 years.

Marginalia

“Weekend” existed 250 years before anyone got Saturdays off.
