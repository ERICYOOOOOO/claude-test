helicopter

Younger than you think
First recorded
1872

≈ 154 years old

As first written

“a spiral wing — Greek helix + pteron”

How it drifted

Coined in French (hélicoptère) for toy models decades before any full-size machine flew (1930s). Chopped illogically into heli-copter, giving us “-copter” everything.

For perspective

That makes helicopter younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 308 years.

Marginalia

The word helicopter is 60 years older than the working helicopter.
