deadline

Younger than you think
First recorded
1864

≈ 162 years old

As first written

“the line around a prison stockade past which prisoners were shot”

How it drifted

Civil War prison camps (notoriously Andersonville) drew a literal dead-line. Printers softened it to a time limit; editors kept the original menace.

For perspective

That makes deadline younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 372 years.

Marginalia

The first deadlines were enforced with rifles.
