dunce

Older than you think
First recorded
1570s

≈ 451 years old

As first written

“a follower of Duns Scotus — once meaning a subtle scholar”

How it drifted

John Duns Scotus was a top medieval philosopher; when humanists turned on scholasticism, “Dunsman” flipped from expert to blockhead. Fame’s cruelest demotion.

For perspective

That makes dunce younger than the printing press (1440) by 135 years.

Marginalia

The dunce cap is named after a genius.
