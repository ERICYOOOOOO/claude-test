gambit

Older than you think
First recorded
1650s

≈ 371 years old

As first written

“a chess opening that sacrifices a pawn — Italian gambetto, “a tripping up””

How it drifted

Italian gambetto, “little leg” — a wrestler’s trip. Chess adopted the trip, then boardrooms adopted the chess.

For perspective

That makes gambit younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 91 years.
