hypocrite

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1200

≈ 826 years old

As first written

“a stage actor — Greek hypokritēs”

How it drifted

Greek hypokritēs meant a performer. Someone acting virtue offstage earned the theatrical label, and the theater lost the word.

For perspective

That makes hypocrite older than the printing press (1440) by 240 years.
