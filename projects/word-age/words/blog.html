blog

Younger than you think
First recorded
1999

≈ 27 years old

As first written

“a weblog — “we blog,” re-cut from “weblog””

How it drifted

Jorn Barger’s “weblog” (1997) was jokingly split as “we blog” by Peter Merholz in 1999. A pun created the word; platforms created the pastime.

For perspective

That makes blog younger than the sinking of the Titanic (1912) by 87 years.
