taxi

Younger than you think
First recorded
1907

≈ 119 years old

As first written

“a taximeter cab — a cab with an automatic fare meter”

How it drifted

From taximètre (French taxe, “tariff”): the meter named the vehicle. Planes “taxi” because early aviators joked they were driving cabs down the runway.

For perspective

That makes taxi younger than the light bulb (1879) by 28 years.
