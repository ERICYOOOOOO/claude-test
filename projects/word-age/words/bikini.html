bikini

Younger than you think
First recorded
1946

≈ 80 years old

As first written

“the swimsuit named after an atomic test site”

How it drifted

Louis Réard named his two-piece days after the U.S. nuclear tests at Bikini Atoll — marketing it as “explosive.” The atoll’s displaced people got no royalties.

For perspective

That makes bikini younger than the Wright brothers’ first flight (1903) by 43 years.
