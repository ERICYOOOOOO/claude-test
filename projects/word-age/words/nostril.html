nostril

Older than you think
First recorded
Old English

≈ 1126 years old

As first written

“nose-hole — nosu + þyrel “hole””

How it drifted

Old English nosþyrl is transparently “nose-thirl,” thirl being a hole. The word for hole died; its fossil lives on in your face.

For perspective

That makes nostril older than Oxford University (c. 1096) by 196 years.
