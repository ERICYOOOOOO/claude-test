cereal

Younger than you think
First recorded
1818

≈ 208 years old

As first written

“of Ceres, goddess of grain — grain crops”

How it drifted

Latin cerealis, “of Ceres.” The breakfast aisle is a shrine to a Roman harvest goddess; the boxed-flakes sense is American, late 1800s.

For perspective

That makes cereal younger than US independence (1776) by 42 years.
