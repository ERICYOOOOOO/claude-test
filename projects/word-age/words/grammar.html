grammar

Older than you think
First recorded
late 14c.

≈ 646 years old

As first written

“learning in general — including, popularly, magic”

How it drifted

Greek grammatikē, “the art of letters.” To the unlettered, Latin learning looked like occult power — a suspicion that split off Scottish “glamour.”

For perspective

That makes grammar older than the printing press (1440) by 60 years.
