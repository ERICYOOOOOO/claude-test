pajamas

Younger than you think
First recorded
c. 1800

≈ 226 years old

As first written

“loose trousers — Persian pāy-jāma, “leg garment””

How it drifted

Daywear across South and West Asia; Europeans adopted them as sleepwear and assumed the word meant bed. The trousers stayed; the shirt was Europe’s addition.

For perspective

That makes pajamas younger than US independence (1776) by 24 years.
