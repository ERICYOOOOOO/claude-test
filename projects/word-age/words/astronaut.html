astronaut

Younger than you think
First recorded
1929

≈ 97 years old

As first written

“a star-sailor — Greek astron + nautēs”

How it drifted

Used in speculative writing three decades before anyone left the atmosphere; modeled on “aeronaut,” the balloonists’ title. The job was named before it existed.

For perspective

That makes astronaut younger than the Wright brothers’ first flight (1903) by 26 years.

Marginalia

“Astronaut” predates spaceflight by 30 years.
