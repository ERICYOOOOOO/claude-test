newt

Older than you think
First recorded
early 15c.

≈ 616 years old

As first written

“an ewt — the n stolen from the article”

How it drifted

The animal was an ewte (Old English efeta, whence also eft); “an ewt” got re-sliced as “a newt.” The opposite theft from apron and umpire.

For perspective

That makes newt older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 82 years.
