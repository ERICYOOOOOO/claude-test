Younger than you think
First recorded
c. 1800

≈ 226 years old

As first written

“a Hindu spiritual teacher — Sanskrit guru, “weighty, venerable””

How it drifted

Sanskrit for “heavy” — a teacher of gravity and substance. The 1960s counterculture imported it; management consulting embalmed it.

For perspective

That makes guru younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 236 years.
