email

Younger than you think
First recorded
1982

≈ 44 years old

As first written

“electronic mail, compressed”

How it drifted

“Electronic mail” shrank to e-mail, then lost the hyphen. The @ in every address was a nearly unused key Ray Tomlinson picked in 1971 because it meant “at.”

For perspective

That makes email younger than the Wright brothers’ first flight (1903) by 79 years.
