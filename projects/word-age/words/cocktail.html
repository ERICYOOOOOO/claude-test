cocktail

Younger than you think
First recorded
1806

≈ 220 years old

As first written

“a bittered sling — spirits, sugar, water, bitters”

How it drifted

First defined in an 1806 New York newspaper. The word’s origin is a famous brawl of theories (docked “cock-tailed” horses? a French egg-cup, coquetier?) — none proven.

For perspective

That makes cocktail younger than US independence (1776) by 30 years.

Marginalia

The first printed definition of “cocktail” (1806) called it an “electioneering potion.”
