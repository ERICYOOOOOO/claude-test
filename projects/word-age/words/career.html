career

Older than you think
First recorded
1530s

≈ 491 years old

As first written

“a racecourse; a gallop at full speed”

How it drifted

French carrière, a horse-racing track (ultimately Latin carrus, wagon). “In full career” meant at top gallop; the professional path kept the breathless pace.

For perspective

That makes career younger than the Black Death reaching England (1348) by 187 years.
