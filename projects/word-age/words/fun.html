fun

Younger than you think
First recorded
1680s

≈ 341 years old

As first written

“a cheat, a trick, a hoax”

How it drifted

“To put the fun upon” someone was to swindle them. The amusement sense emerged in the 1700s — and was condemned as vulgar slang by Samuel Johnson’s circle.

For perspective

That makes fun younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 193 years.

Marginalia

Shakespeare never had fun. The word arrived seven decades after his death — and meant fraud.
