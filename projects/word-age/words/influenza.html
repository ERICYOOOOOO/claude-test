influenza

Younger than you think
First recorded
1743

≈ 283 years old

As first written

“the influence (of the stars) — Italian for epidemic”

How it drifted

Italians blamed epidemics on celestial influenza. English adopted the word during the outbreak of 1743, and clipped it to “flu” by the mid-1800s.

For perspective

That makes influenza younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 179 years.
