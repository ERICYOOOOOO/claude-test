safari

Younger than you think
First recorded
1859

≈ 167 years old

As first written

“a journey, an expedition — Swahili, from Arabic safar”

How it drifted

Swahili safari simply means “journey.” English explorers narrowed it to the gun-and-khaki kind; Windows narrowed it further to a browser’s rival.

For perspective

That makes safari exactly as old as Darwin’s On the Origin of Species (1859).
