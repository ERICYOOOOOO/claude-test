penguin

Older than you think
First recorded
1570s

≈ 451 years old

As first written

“probably the great auk of the North Atlantic”

How it drifted

Sailors first used it for the (now extinct) great auk; the name — perhaps Welsh pen gwyn, “white head” — was reassigned to the southern lookalike.

For perspective

That makes penguin younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 83 years.
