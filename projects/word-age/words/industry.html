industry

Older than you think
First recorded
late 15c.

≈ 546 years old

As first written

“diligence, hard work — a personal virtue”

How it drifted

Latin industria, “diligence.” It was a trait (“she showed great industry”) before it was a sector; the smokestacks borrowed a character reference.

For perspective

That makes industry older than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 84 years.
