hurricane

Older than you think
First recorded
1550s

≈ 471 years old

As first written

“the great storm — Taíno hurakán”

How it drifted

From the Taíno storm word (possibly a storm deity), via Spanish huracán. One of the Caribbean’s first exports to global English.

For perspective

That makes hurricane younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 63 years.
