ketchup

Older than you think
First recorded
1680s

≈ 341 years old

As first written

“a fermented fish sauce — Hokkien kê-tsiap”

How it drifted

Southeast Asian fish brine, via Malay and Hokkien. English cooks made mushroom and walnut ketchups for a century; tomatoes only captured the bottle in the 1800s.

For perspective

That makes ketchup younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 121 years.

Marginalia

The original ketchup contained fish, and no tomatoes whatsoever.
