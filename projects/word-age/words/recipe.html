recipe

Older than you think
First recorded
1580s

≈ 441 years old

As first written

“a doctor’s prescription — Latin “take!””

How it drifted

Recipe is the Latin imperative “take,” opening every prescription (abbreviated ℞). Cooking borrowed the pharmacist’s format in the 1700s.

For perspective

That makes recipe younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 21 years.

Marginalia

The pharmacy symbol ℞ and your cookie recipe are the same word.
