computer

Older than you think
First recorded
1640s

≈ 381 years old

As first written

“a person who computes — a human calculator”

How it drifted

For three centuries a computer was a job title; NASA still employed human computers in the 1960s. The machines took the name along with the work.

For perspective

That makes computer younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 81 years.

Marginalia

“Computer” was a profession for 300 years before it was a device.
