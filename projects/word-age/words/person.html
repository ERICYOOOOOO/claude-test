person

Older than you think
First recorded
early 13c.

≈ 806 years old

As first written

“a human being — from persona, an actor’s mask”

How it drifted

Latin persona was the mask worn on stage, then the character, then the individual. Every person is, etymologically, a role being performed.

For perspective

That makes person older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 272 years.
