hitchhike

Younger than you think
First recorded
1921

≈ 105 years old

As first written

“to travel by begging rides”

How it drifted

American coinage from hitch (fasten on) + hike. The automobile created the practice, the word, and eventually the horror-movie genre about it.

For perspective

That makes hitchhike younger than the Wright brothers’ first flight (1903) by 18 years.
