pandemic

Older than you think
First recorded
1660s

≈ 361 years old

As first written

“affecting all peoples — Greek pan + dēmos”

How it drifted

Greek pandēmos, “of all the people.” The word waited three and a half centuries in medical texts for its terrible mainstream debut.

For perspective

That makes pandemic younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 173 years.

Marginalia

“Pandemic” is older than the United States by over a century.
