leotard

Younger than you think
First recorded
1886

≈ 140 years old

As first written

“the skin-tight costume of acrobat Jules Léotard”

How it drifted

Léotard invented the flying trapeze act and the garment to perform it in; the name attached posthumously. He also inspired “The Daring Young Man on the Flying Trapeze.”

For perspective

That makes leotard younger than US independence (1776) by 110 years.
