candidate

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1600

≈ 426 years old

As first written

“one clothed in white — Latin candidatus”

How it drifted

Roman office-seekers wore togas whitened with chalk to signal purity. The costume named the applicant; the purity remains aspirational.

For perspective

That makes candidate younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 108 years.
