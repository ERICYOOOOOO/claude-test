shark

Older than you think
First recorded
1560s

≈ 457 years old

As first written

“the great sea fish — origin unknown”

How it drifted

Popularized when Captain Hawkins’s crew exhibited one in London in 1569; where the sailors got the word remains one of etymology’s open cases.

For perspective

That makes shark younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 77 years.

Marginalia

Nobody knows where “shark” comes from. The card shark may be from a different word entirely (German Schurke, “scoundrel”).
