hi

Younger than you think
First recorded
1862

≈ 164 years old

As first written

“an exclamation to hail someone”

How it drifted

An American variant of the older hail-call “hey/hi!” recorded as a greeting in the 1860s. The friendliest word in English is barely 160 years old.

For perspective

That makes hi younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 298 years.
