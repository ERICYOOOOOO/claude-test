trousers

Older than you think
First recorded
1610s

≈ 411 years old

As first written

“Gaelic close-fitting leg garments — triubhas”

How it drifted

From Irish/Scottish Gaelic triubhas (“trews”). Mocked as Celtic dress, then standard issue: the empire adopted the garment it laughed at.

For perspective

That makes trousers younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 51 years.
