jazz

Younger than you think
First recorded
1912

≈ 114 years old

As first written

“pep, energy — West Coast baseball slang”

How it drifted

First printed in baseball reporting (“jazz ball”); attached to the New Orleans music by 1915. Its deeper origin is the most-fought-over mystery in American etymology.

For perspective

That makes jazz younger than the light bulb (1879) by 33 years.

Marginalia

“Jazz” first appears in print describing a baseball pitch, not music.
