satellite

Older than you think
First recorded
1540s

≈ 478 years old

As first written

“an attendant; a hanger-on of an important person”

How it drifted

Latin satelles, a bodyguard-follower. Kepler applied it to moons orbiting planets (1610s); Sputnik made the courtier mechanical in 1957.

For perspective

That makes satellite younger than the printing press (1440) by 108 years.
