decimate

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1600

≈ 426 years old

As first written

“to execute one in ten — the Roman army punishment”

How it drifted

Latin decimare: mutinous legions drew lots and every tenth man was killed. Modern use for near-total destruction reverses the arithmetic, to pedants’ endless grief.

For perspective

That makes decimate younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 36 years.
