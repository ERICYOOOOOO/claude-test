commute

Younger than you think
First recorded
1889

≈ 137 years old

As first written

“to travel on a commutation ticket — a season pass”

How it drifted

Railroads “commuted” (converted) daily fares into one season ticket; holders were commuters. The discount named the daily grind.

For perspective

That makes commute exactly as old as the Eiffel Tower (1889).
