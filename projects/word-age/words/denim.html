denim

Older than you think
First recorded
1690s

≈ 331 years old

As first written

“serge de Nîmes — fabric of Nîmes, France”

How it drifted

“Serge de Nîmes” wore down to “denim.” Jeans, likewise, are from Gênes — French for Genoa. Your jeans are two French place names sewn together.

For perspective

That makes denim younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 131 years.
