volcano

Older than you think
First recorded
1610s

≈ 416 years old

As first written

“the forge-mountain of Vulcan”

How it drifted

Italian volcano, from Vulcanus, the Roman smith-god whose forge rumbled under Etna. Geology kept the god on the letterhead.

For perspective

That makes volcano younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 46 years.
