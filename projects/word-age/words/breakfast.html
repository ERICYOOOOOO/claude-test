breakfast

Younger than you think
First recorded
mid-15c.

≈ 576 years old

As first written

“the meal that breaks the night’s fast”

How it drifted

Exactly what it says: break + fast. The surprise is how late it is — English did without a word for the morning meal until the 1400s.

For perspective

That makes breakfast older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 42 years.
