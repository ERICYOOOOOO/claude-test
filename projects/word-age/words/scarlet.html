scarlet

Older than you think
First recorded
mid-13c.

≈ 776 years old

As first written

“a rich cloth — of any color, even blue or green”

How it drifted

Scarlet was a luxury fabric first; since the most prized bolts were dyed vivid red, the textile’s name bled into the color.

For perspective

That makes scarlet older than the printing press (1440) by 190 years.
