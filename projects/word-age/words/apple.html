apple

Older than you think
First recorded
Old English

≈ 1126 years old

As first written

“fruit in general; any fruit”

How it drifted

Old English æppel could name almost any fruit — even dates were “finger-apples.” It narrowed to one orchard species late.

For perspective

That makes apple older than Oxford University (c. 1096) by 196 years.

Marginalia

Genesis never names the forbidden fruit; “apple” filled the blank because apple once meant fruit.
