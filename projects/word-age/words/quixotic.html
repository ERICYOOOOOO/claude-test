Younger than you think
First recorded
1718

≈ 308 years old

As first written

“like Don Quixote — nobly deluded”

How it drifted

Cervantes’s knight tilted at windmills in 1605; within a century his name was an English adjective for gallant impracticality.

For perspective

That makes quixotic younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 154 years.
