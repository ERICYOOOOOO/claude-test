sarcasm

Older than you think
First recorded
1570s

≈ 451 years old

As first written

“a flesh-stripping taunt — Greek sarkazein”

How it drifted

Greek sarkazein: “to tear flesh like a dog.” The Greeks classified cutting speech as literal butchery. Modern usage has not softened the anatomy.

For perspective

That makes sarcasm younger than the printing press (1440) by 135 years.
