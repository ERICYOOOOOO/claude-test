pie

Older than you think
First recorded
mid-14c.

≈ 676 years old

As first written

“a dish of mixed ingredients in pastry”

How it drifted

Origin uncertain — perhaps from magpie (Latin pica), the bird that collects odds and ends, since early pies held miscellany the way the bird’s nest did.

For perspective

That makes pie older than the printing press (1440) by 90 years.
