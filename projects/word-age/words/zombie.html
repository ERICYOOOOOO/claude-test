Younger than you think
First recorded
1871

≈ 155 years old

As first written

“a reanimated corpse in Haitian and West African belief”

How it drifted

From a West African word (compare Kongo nzambi, “god, spirit”) via Haitian Creole. Hollywood found it in the 1930s and it has shambled through pop culture since.

For perspective

That makes zombie younger than US independence (1776) by 95 years.
