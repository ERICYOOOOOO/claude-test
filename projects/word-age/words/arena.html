arena

Older than you think
First recorded
1620s

≈ 401 years old

As first written

“sand — the floor of the amphitheater”

How it drifted

Latin harena, “sand,” strewn to soak up blood between bouts. The absorbent flooring became the venue, then the abstraction (“the political arena”).

For perspective

That makes arena younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 61 years.
