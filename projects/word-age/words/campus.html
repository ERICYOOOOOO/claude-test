campus

Younger than you think
First recorded
1774

≈ 252 years old

As first written

“the field of a college — first at Princeton”

How it drifted

Latin campus, “field.” First applied to Princeton’s grounds; now every office park with a cafeteria borrows the academic lawn.

For perspective

That makes campus younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 210 years.
