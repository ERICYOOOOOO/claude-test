nausea

Older than you think
First recorded
early 15c.

≈ 616 years old

As first written

“seasickness — Greek naus, “ship””

How it drifted

Greek nausia: ship-sickness, from naus (as in nautical). Every wave of nausea is etymologically a boat ride.

For perspective

That makes nausea older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 82 years.
