journey

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1200

≈ 826 years old

As first written

“a day’s travel; a day’s work”

How it drifted

From Old French journée “a day.” A journey was what you could ride between dawn and dusk — about 20 miles. The day limit fell away; the word kept walking.

For perspective

That makes journey older than the printing press (1440) by 240 years.
