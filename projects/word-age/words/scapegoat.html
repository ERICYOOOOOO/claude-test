scapegoat

Older than you think
First recorded
1530

≈ 496 years old

As first written

“the goat sent into the wilderness bearing the people’s sins”

How it drifted

Coined by William Tyndale for his Bible translation (Leviticus 16): the escape-goat. The ritual animal became everyone unfairly blamed since.

For perspective

That makes scapegoat younger than the printing press (1440) by 90 years.
