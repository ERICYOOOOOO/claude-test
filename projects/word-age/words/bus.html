bus

Younger than you think
First recorded
1832

≈ 194 years old

As first written

“an omnibus — Latin “for everyone””

How it drifted

Latin omnibus, “for all,” clipped to its meaningless tail end: -bus is just a case ending. Grammarians grumbled; passengers boarded anyway.

For perspective

That makes bus younger than US independence (1776) by 56 years.

Marginalia

“Bus” is a Latin suffix driving around without its word.
