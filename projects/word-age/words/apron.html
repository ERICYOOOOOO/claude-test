apron

Older than you think
First recorded
mid-15c.

≈ 576 years old

As first written

“a napron — from French naperon, “little tablecloth””

How it drifted

“A napron” was mis-divided as “an apron,” and the n migrated into the article. Napkin and napery kept the lost letter.

For perspective

That makes apron older than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 114 years.
