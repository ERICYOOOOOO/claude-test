gentle

Older than you think
First recorded
early 13c.

≈ 816 years old

As first written

“well-born, noble of family”

How it drifted

From Latin gentilis, “of the same clan.” Good birth was assumed to mean good manners; “gentle” slid from pedigree to temperament. Gentleman kept both books.

For perspective

That makes gentle older than the printing press (1440) by 230 years.
