lure

Older than you think
First recorded
early 14c.

≈ 696 years old

As first written

“a falconer’s device to recall the hawk”

How it drifted

The lure was a bundle of feathers on a cord, swung to bring a falcon back to the fist. Temptation has been on a string ever since.

For perspective

That makes lure older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 162 years.
