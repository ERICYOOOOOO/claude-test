airplane

Younger than you think
First recorded
1907

≈ 119 years old

As first written

“a powered heavier-than-air flying machine”

How it drifted

Replaced “aeroplane” in American use. The Wright brothers themselves said “flying machine” — their 1903 patent doesn’t contain “airplane” at all.

For perspective

That makes airplane younger than US independence (1776) by 131 years.

Marginalia

“Aeroplane” (1866) is older than powered flight by 37 years — coined for designs that couldn’t yet fly.
