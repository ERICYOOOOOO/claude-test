dragon

Older than you think
First recorded
early 13c.

≈ 806 years old

As first written

“a huge serpent — Greek drakōn”

How it drifted

Greek drakōn, a great serpent, likely from a root meaning “to see clearly” — the sharp-eyed one. Wings and fire were added by later management.

For perspective

That makes dragon older than the printing press (1440) by 220 years.
