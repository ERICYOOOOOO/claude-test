saxophone

Younger than you think
First recorded
1851

≈ 175 years old

As first written

“the horn of Adolphe Sax”

How it drifted

Belgian instrument maker Adolphe Sax patented it in 1846; English took the name by 1851. Jazz was still half a century away.

For perspective

That makes saxophone younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 287 years.
