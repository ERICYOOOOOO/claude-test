bank

Older than you think
First recorded
late 15c.

≈ 546 years old

As first written

“a money-changer’s bench”

How it drifted

Italian banca, the bench where Renaissance money-changers did business. Break the bench — banca rotta — and you were bankrupt.

For perspective

That makes bank younger than the printing press (1440) by 40 years.
