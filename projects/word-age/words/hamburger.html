hamburger

Younger than you think
First recorded
1884

≈ 142 years old

As first written

“Hamburg steak — chopped beef, Hamburg style”

How it drifted

Named for the German port, via its emigrants’ chopped beef. “Burger” was then amputated to breed cheeseburgers — ham was never an ingredient.

For perspective

That makes hamburger younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 320 years.

Marginalia

There is no ham in a hamburger. There is a German city.
