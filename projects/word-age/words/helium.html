helium

Younger than you think
First recorded
1868

≈ 158 years old

As first written

“the sun element — found in sunlight before on Earth”

How it drifted

Spotted as a yellow line in the sun’s spectrum during an eclipse, so named for Helios. It was discovered 93 million miles away, 27 years before anyone found it here.

For perspective

That makes helium younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 376 years.

Marginalia

Helium was discovered on the sun first. Earth caught up in 1895.
