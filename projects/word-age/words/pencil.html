pencil

Older than you think
First recorded
mid-14c.

≈ 676 years old

As first written

“an artist’s fine brush — Latin penicillus, “little tail””

How it drifted

A pencil was a small camel-hair brush; graphite sticks inherited the name in the 1500s. Penicillin comes from the same “little brush” — the mold’s shape.

For perspective

That makes pencil older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 142 years.
