porcupine

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1400

≈ 626 years old

As first written

“the spine-pig — porc espin”

How it drifted

Old French porc espin, “thorn pig.” English spelling thrashed for centuries (Shakespeare wrote “porpentine”) before settling.

For perspective

That makes porcupine older than the printing press (1440) by 40 years.
