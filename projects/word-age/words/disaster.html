disaster

Older than you think
First recorded
1590s

≈ 431 years old

As first written

“an ill-starred event — Italian disastro, “bad star””

How it drifted

Dis- + astro: born under a bad star. Astrology’s verdict on catastrophe fossilized into the word after the horoscopes were forgotten.

For perspective

That makes disaster younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 31 years.
