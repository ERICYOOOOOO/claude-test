gin

Younger than you think
First recorded
1714

≈ 312 years old

As first written

“geneva, clipped — the juniper spirit”

How it drifted

From Dutch genever, “juniper” (nothing to do with the Swiss city). London shortened the word and multiplied the consumption — the Gin Craze followed.

For perspective

That makes gin younger than the printing press (1440) by 274 years.
