steward

Older than you think
First recorded
Old English

≈ 1126 years old

As first written

“keeper of the hall — literally “sty-ward””

How it drifted

Old English stigweard joins stig (hall or pen) to weard “guard.” From pig-yard keeper to estate manager to royal dynasty (the Stewarts) to flight attendant.

For perspective

That makes steward older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 592 years.
