galvanize

Younger than you think
First recorded
1801

≈ 225 years old

As first written

“to stimulate with electric current — after Luigi Galvani”

How it drifted

Galvani made dead frogs’ legs kick with electricity. Rousing anything inert — metal coatings, committees — now borrows his name.

For perspective

That makes galvanize younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 237 years.
