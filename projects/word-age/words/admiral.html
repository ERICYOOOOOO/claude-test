admiral

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1200

≈ 826 years old

As first written

“an emir; a Saracen commander”

How it drifted

From Arabic amīr “commander.” Crusaders brought home the title; scribes glued on the -al of Latin admirabilis, and the sea claimed it.

For perspective

That makes admiral older than the Magna Carta (1215) by 15 years.
