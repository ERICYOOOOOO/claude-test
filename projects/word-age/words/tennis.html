tennis

Older than you think
First recorded
mid-14c.

≈ 676 years old

As first written

“the game itself — probably from French tenez!, “take it!””

How it drifted

Most likely from the server’s cry tenez — “receive!” The medieval game was palm-against-ball; rackets, lawns, and Wimbledon came much later.

For perspective

That makes tennis older than the printing press (1440) by 90 years.
