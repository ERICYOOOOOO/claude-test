curfew

Older than you think
First recorded
early 14c.

≈ 696 years old

As first written

“the evening bell to cover fires — couvre-feu”

How it drifted

Old French cuevrefeu, “cover fire”: the bell ordering hearths damped for the night so towns wouldn’t burn. The fire safety became social control.

For perspective

That makes curfew older than the printing press (1440) by 110 years.
