smile

Younger than you think
First recorded
c. 1300

≈ 726 years old

As first written

“to smile — a newcomer replacing older “smirk””

How it drifted

Probably from Scandinavian. Before it arrived, Old English used smearcian — smirk — for every smile. The friendly word is the young one.

For perspective

That makes smile older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 192 years.

Marginalia

English could smirk for centuries before it learned to smile.
