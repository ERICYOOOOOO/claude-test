tsunami

Younger than you think
First recorded
1896

≈ 130 years old

As first written

“harbor wave — Japanese tsu + nami”

How it drifted

Entered English with reports of Japan’s 1896 Sanriku disaster. Adopted by science because “tidal wave” was wrong — tides have nothing to do with it.

For perspective

That makes tsunami younger than the telephone patent (1876) by 20 years.
