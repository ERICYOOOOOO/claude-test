dessert

Older than you think
First recorded
c. 1600

≈ 426 years old

As first written

“the clearing of the table — French desservir”

How it drifted

French desservir, “to un-serve”: what arrived after the dishes were removed. The course is named for the tablecloth being swept of crumbs.

For perspective

That makes dessert younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 36 years.
