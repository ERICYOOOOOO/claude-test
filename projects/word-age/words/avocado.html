avocado

Older than you think
First recorded
1690s

≈ 331 years old

As first written

“Nahuatl āhuacatl — reshaped by Spanish folk etymology”

How it drifted

Spanish speakers bent the Nahuatl name toward their own word abogado (“lawyer”) and aguacate; English took “avocado.” Guacamole keeps the original more faithfully.

For perspective

That makes avocado younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 131 years.
