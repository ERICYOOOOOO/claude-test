tremendous

Older than you think
First recorded
1630s

≈ 391 years old

As first written

“dreadful — that which makes one tremble”

How it drifted

Latin tremendus: “to be trembled at.” The shudder was upgraded to applause by the same inflation that flipped awful and terrific.

For perspective

That makes tremendous younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 71 years.
