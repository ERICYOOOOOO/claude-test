ghost

Older than you think
First recorded
Old English

≈ 1126 years old

As first written

“breath; the soul, the spirit of life”

How it drifted

Old English gāst was breath and soul — the Holy Ghost keeps the old sense. The haunting specter is the junior meaning.

For perspective

That makes ghost older than Oxford University (c. 1096) by 196 years.
