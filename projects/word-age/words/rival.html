rival

Older than you think
First recorded
1570s

≈ 451 years old

As first written

“one who shares the same stream”

How it drifted

Latin rivalis: a neighbor drawing water from the same rivus (brook). People who share water compete over it — the law of every riverbank became a word.

For perspective

That makes rival younger than the Black Death reaching England (1348) by 227 years.
