jaywalk

Younger than you think
First recorded
1912

≈ 114 years old

As first written

“to cross the street like a “jay” — a country simpleton”

How it drifted

Jay was slang for a rube dazzled by city traffic. Motor lobbies pushed the word to shift blame from cars to walkers — an insult with a marketing budget.

For perspective

That makes jaywalk younger than the light bulb (1879) by 33 years.
