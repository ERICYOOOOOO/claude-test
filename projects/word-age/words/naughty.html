naughty

Older than you think
First recorded
late 14c.

≈ 646 years old

As first written

“needy; having naught, owning nothing”

How it drifted

Naught-y: possessing nothing. From “poor” it slid to “morally worthless” to “wicked” — then softened until only children and desserts qualify.

For perspective

That makes naughty older than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 112 years.
