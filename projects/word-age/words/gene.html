Younger than you think
First recorded
1911

≈ 115 years old

As first written

“the unit of heredity — Danish gen, from Greek genos”

How it drifted

Coined by Wilhelm Johannsen (1909, Danish) decades before anyone knew genes were DNA. A word that named a thing nobody had seen.

For perspective

That makes gene younger than the telephone patent (1876) by 35 years.
