dude

Younger than you think
First recorded
1883

≈ 143 years old

As first written

“a fastidious dandy — New York slang”

How it drifted

1883 newspaper slang for overdressed young swells (possibly from “Yankee Doodle”). Dude ranches sold them the West; surfers democratized the word.

For perspective

That makes dude younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 391 years.

Marginalia

The first dudes were Victorian fashion victims.
