workaholic

Younger than you think
First recorded
1968

≈ 58 years old

As first written

“one addicted to work — on the model of alcoholic”

How it drifted

Popularized by pastor-psychologist Wayne Oates. The -aholic suffix then metastasized: chocoholic, shopaholic — a piece of “alcohol” glued to everything.

For perspective

That makes workaholic younger than the sinking of the Titanic (1912) by 56 years.
