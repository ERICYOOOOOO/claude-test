cute

Younger than you think
First recorded
1731

≈ 295 years old

As first written

“clever, sharp — a clipping of “acute””

How it drifted

’Cute was acute with the head chopped off: shrewd. American English drifted it to “pretty” in the 1830s; the cunning still flickers in “don’t get cute.”

For perspective

That makes cute younger than Shakespeare’s birth (1564) by 167 years.
