hashtag

Younger than you think
First recorded
2007

≈ 19 years old

As first written

“a #-labeled topic — proposed in a tweet”

How it drifted

Chris Messina suggested # for Twitter groups in August 2007. The # itself is ancient bookkeeping (the “pound sign,” from libra); the word is barely adult.

For perspective

That makes hashtag exactly as old as the iPhone (2007).
