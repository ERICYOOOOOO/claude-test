harvest

Older than you think
First recorded
Old English

≈ 1126 years old

As first written

“autumn, the season itself”

How it drifted

Old English hærfest named the season. When “autumn” and “fall” moved in, harvest kept only the season’s work — the gathering.

For perspective

That makes harvest older than Oxford University (c. 1096) by 196 years.

Marginalia

German Herbst still means “autumn.”
