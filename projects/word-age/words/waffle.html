waffle

Younger than you think
First recorded
1744

≈ 282 years old

As first written

“the gridded batter cake — Dutch wafel”

How it drifted

From Dutch wafel (kin to “wafer” and “weave” — the grid is a woven pattern). The verb waffle, “to talk on evasively,” is unrelated — from a word for yelping.

For perspective

That makes waffle younger than Columbus’s first voyage (1492) by 252 years.
