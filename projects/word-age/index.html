      Method

      Dates follow the standard etymological references (the conventions of the OED and Etymonline). “c.” means circa; “Old English” means before about 1150 — we do not invent precision the record doesn’t have.

      Words with uncertain histories are either flagged as such or left out. If a story sounds too good to be true (NEWS is not an acronym; “sincere” has no wax in it), we say so.
